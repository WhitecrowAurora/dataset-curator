# 密集文字重打规则

`prompts/` 下所有 `.md` 会被合并读取，**同名节取文件名排序靠前的那一份**。
所以这份文件只能写 `CAPTION_DENSE` 系列节名，绝不能出现 `CAPTION` / `TEXT`
这类通用节——那会把 `打标规则.md` 与 `图中文字规则.md` 里的正式规则顶掉，
而现象是"正常路径的 prompt 悄悄换成了收窄版"，没有任何报错。

## CAPTION_DENSE
- One paragraph of {char_lo}-{char_hi} characters, on a single line. Stop at the upper bound.
- Order the content: main subject; setting; composition and camera angle; lighting; any prominent visible text, quoted verbatim inside double quotes.
- Name only the figures and the setting that carry the image. Background objects, decorations and repeated props get one summing clause at most.
- Comic panels and speech-bubble scenes: say how many bubbles there are, roughly where they sit, and what the main one says. Do not narrate each bubble in turn and do not retell the dialogue.
- Name the colour and surface of the main subjects. Skip colour for anything that only appears in passing.
- Name the overall art style and medium in one clause.
- Do not name or guess the artist, character, series or studio.
- Every sentence must add a new fact. Drop any sentence that only restates an earlier one.
- English only, plain prose: no markdown, no bullet points, no headings, no prefixes.

## CAPTION_DENSE_ZH
- 一段连续的中文散文，约 {char_lo}-{char_hi} 个汉字，单行、不换行。写到上限就停。
- 内容顺序：主体；场景；构图与机位；光线；画面里显著的文字，用双引号原样抄下来。
- 只写撑起这张图的人物与场景。背景物件、装饰、重复道具最多一句话带过。
- 有对话气泡的分镜：说明有几处气泡、大致在什么位置、主要那一处写了什么即可。不逐格叙述，不复述对白。
- 主要主体给出颜色与表面质感；只是顺带出现的物件不必逐个配色。
- 用一句话说明整体画风与媒介。
- 不要点名或猜测画师、角色、系列、工作室。
- 每一句都要增加一个新事实；只是在重述前一句的，删掉。
- 纯散文：不要 markdown、不要分条、不要小标题、不要任何前缀。
