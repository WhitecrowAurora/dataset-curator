# 密集文字重打规则

`prompts/` 下所有 `.md` 会被合并读取，**同名节取文件名排序靠前的那一份**。
所以这份文件只能写 `CAPTION_DENSE` 系列节名，绝不能出现 `CAPTION` / `TEXT`
这类通用节——那会把 `打标规则.md` 与 `图中文字规则.md` 里的正式规则顶掉，
而现象是"正常路径的 prompt 悄悄换成了收窄版"，没有任何报错。

## CAPTION_DENSE
- One paragraph of {char_lo}-{char_hi} characters, on a single line. Treat the character range as guidance and finish complete sentences within the training-token budget.
- Order the content: main subject; setting; composition and camera angle when clear; lighting. Keep the focus on the visible scene.
- Name only the figures and the setting that carry the image. Background objects, decorations and repeated props get one summing clause at most.
- Comic panels and speech bubbles: mention their presence in one brief clause when visually important. Do not count every bubble, transcribe or retell dialogue, or list tiny and repeated text. If text itself is the main subject, quote at most one short, prominent, clearly legible phrase in its original script; omit illegible text without placeholders.
- Mention colour and surface details for main subjects only when clearly visible and useful. Omit uncertain details; do not infer material from colour or lighting.
- When clearly distinguishable, name the overall art style and medium in one clause.
- Do not name or guess the artist, character, series or studio.
- Every sentence must add a new fact. Drop any sentence that only restates an earlier one.
- English only, plain prose: no markdown, no bullet points, no headings, no prefixes.

## CAPTION_DENSE_ZH
- 一段连续的中文散文，约 {char_lo}-{char_hi} 个汉字，单行、不换行。字数区间仅作引导，在训练 token 预算内完整结束句子。
- 内容顺序：主体；场景；构图，以及清楚可辨时的机位；光线。重点始终放在可见画面上。
- 只写撑起这张图的人物与场景。背景物件、装饰、重复道具最多一句话带过。
- 分镜与对话气泡只有影响画面构成时才用一个短句概括其存在。不逐个数气泡、不抄写或复述对白、不罗列小字和重复文字。文字本身就是主体时，最多原样引用一处简短、显著且清楚可辨的文字；看不清的直接省略，不写占位符。
- 主要主体的颜色与表面质感只在清晰可见且有用时说明；不确定时省略，不要根据颜色或光影推断材质。
- 用一句话说明清楚可辨的整体画风与媒介。
- 不要点名或猜测画师、角色、系列、工作室。
- 每一句都要增加一个新事实；只是在重述前一句的，删掉。
- 纯散文：不要 markdown、不要分条、不要小标题、不要任何前缀。
