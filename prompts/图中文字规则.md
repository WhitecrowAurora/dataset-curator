# 图中文字规则

这一份管"画面里的字怎么描述"，与 `打标规则.md` 里的 TAG / CAPTION 分开，
方便单独迭代措辞（加字体风格、加"认不准别猜"之类）。

- `## TEXT` —— 英文 caption 用
- `## TEXT_ZH` —— 中文 caption 用

只有 caption 出英文时追加 TEXT，出中文时追加 TEXT_ZH。两份讲的事必须
一致，否则中英数据分布会分叉。

`tiers.yaml` 的 `output.text_rules` 控制是否追加这一份；tag-only 模式下
本来就不写 caption，这一份不会被加载。

## TEXT

- Transcribe only text that is prominent, fully legible, and clearly part of the main subject: brand names, product names, shop signs, titles, headlines, and large labels. Quote it verbatim inside double quotes.
- Do not try to be exhaustive. Leave out text that is small, dense, secondary or incidental: ingredient lists, nutrition tables, fine print, legal boilerplate, serial and batch numbers, prices in a dense grid, barcodes, text that sits inside or beside a small icon or badge, and text on out-of-focus or background objects. Omitting these is correct; only the main readable text is wanted.
- If a piece of text is only partly legible, or transcribing it would require guessing, inferring or completing any character, omit that text entirely and do not mention it. Never supply a plausible word, brand or product name to fill a gap. An incomplete caption is fine; an invented transcript is not.
- Text visible in the image keeps its own language and script inside the quotes, even though this paragraph is in English: never translate it, never romanise or transliterate it. A quoted Chinese or Japanese sign is correct as-is. Do not add a reading, pinyin, romaji, translation or pronunciation in brackets after the quote — write `the sign reads "欢迎光临"`, never `"欢迎光临" (huānyíng guānglín)`.
- For the text you do transcribe, describe the typeface as well: print or handwriting, serif or sans-serif, weight, italic, outlined or shadowed, glossy or matte, relative size, and how worn or fresh it looks (e.g. "handwritten brush script", "heavy sans-serif", "faint grey watermark", "faded printed label").

## TEXT_ZH

- 只抄画面主体上显著、完整、一眼就能认出的文字：品牌名、商品名、店招、标题、大标签。用双引号原样抄下来。
- 不要试图抄全。小而密、次要或顺带出现的文字一律略过：配料表、营养成分表、小字说明、免责条款、序列号和批号、密集排列的价格、条形码、小图标或徽章里面和旁边的字、虚化背景上的字。略过这些是正确的，只要主体上那些看得清的字。
- 一处文字如果认不全，或者抄下来需要猜、需要推断、需要补全任何一个字，就整条不写，也不要提它。绝不拿一个看起来合理的词、品牌名或商品名去填空——漏标没关系，编造的抄录不能有。
- 引号里的文字保持它原本的语言和字形，不要翻译、不要音译或罗马化；正文用简体中文，引文可以不是中文。引号后面不要加注音、拼音、罗马字、译文或读音，写成「招牌上写着"欢迎光临"」，不要写成「"欢迎光临"（huānyíng guānglín）」。
- 对你确实抄下来的文字，连同它的字体风格一起描述：印刷体还是手写、衬线还是无衬线、粗细、是否斜体、是否描边或带投影、是否反光、字号大小、新旧或磨损程度（例如"手写楷体""加粗无衬线""浅灰水印""褪色的印刷体"）。
