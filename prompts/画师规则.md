# 画师规则

约束「画师名」怎么进正文。画师名是标识符，不是描述：它是训练侧把人绑到作品的键，
写错一个字符就等于绑不上。规则分英文与中文两节，按这次正文的语言取用；
模板里的 `{a}` / `{rest}` 是占位符，不是正文内容。

## ARTIST_EN
- An artist name is an identifier, not a description: keep it character-for-character. Never translate it, transliterate it, split it, or change its case, spacing, hyphenation or script.
- If this prompt shows an artist as a placeholder token (`__ARTIST_0__`, `__ARTIST_1__`, ...), copy that token back exactly as written: do not add `@`, do not translate it, and do not write a name next to it.
- Do not write the artist sentence yourself: the annotation system prepends it, and the caption you write begins right after its comma.
- The prepended sentence always has the same shape: the template `In the style of @{a}, {rest}` — `{a}` is the artist name, `{rest}` is your caption. Those braces are placeholders for the system, not text: never copy the template, its braces or any part of the prefix into the caption.
- Never start the caption with the artist name alone, with or without `@`, and never with a name followed only by punctuation: the first thing in the caption is the prepended template sentence, and your text continues after its comma. Do not repeat that sentence, and do not move it to the middle or the end.
- When no trustworthy artist is given, omit the artist sentence entirely: never invent a name and never write "possibly by ..." or "in the style of a similar artist".
- Do not carry tag-type labels into the caption: no `artist:`, `character:`, `general:`, `meta:`, and no `(artist tag)`-style brackets.
- Text visible in the image stays verbatim in its original script; a date or year visible in the image is quoted only as visible text and never used to date the artwork or its style.

## ARTIST_ZH
- 画师名是标识符不是描述：逐字保留，不翻译、不音译、不拆词，也不改大小写、空格、连字符或书写系统。
- 提示里若出现占位符形式的画师（`__ARTIST_0__`、`__ARTIST_1__`…），原样抄回：不要加 `@`、不要翻译、也不要在旁边另写名字。
- 不要自己写画师句：标注系统会把它放在正文最开头，你写的正文从它那个逗号之后开始。
- 那句放进来的话形状固定：模板 `使用 @{a} 的风格，{rest}`——`{a}` 是画师名，`{rest}` 是你写的正文。花括号是给系统填的占位符，不是正文：不要把模板本身、花括号或任何前缀抄进正文。
- 正文开头不能只有画师名（带不带 `@` 都不行），也不能是「名字 + 标点」：正文的第一句就是被放进来的模板句，你的文字接在它逗号之后。不要重复那句话，也不要把它挪到中段或句尾。
- 没有可信画师时整句省略：不造名字，不写「疑似某画师」「风格类似某画师」这类说法。
- 不要把 tag 类型标注夹进正文：不写 `artist:`、`character:`、`general:`、`meta:`，也不写 `（artist tag）` 这类括注。
- 图内文字按原文保留；图内出现的日期或年份只作为可见文字引用，不用于推断作品年代或画风年代。
