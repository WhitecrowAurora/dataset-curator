# 图中文字规则

只在打开 `text_rules` 时追加，约束「图里出现的文字」怎么处理，不要让它污染画面描述本身。

## TEXT
- Quote only short, clearly legible text that is prominent and useful for understanding the image, verbatim in its original script.
- never translate the text, and never romanise non-Latin scripts.
- Keep original punctuation and reading order within a quotation. Omit illegible text; never guess missing characters or add [unreadable] placeholders.
- For dense dialogue, many speech bubbles, repeated labels or tiny background print, mention their presence in one brief clause if visually important; do not transcribe or retell their contents, count every bubble or list each text region. If text itself is the main subject, quote at most one short, prominent, clearly legible phrase.
- Do not describe the font or layout unless the text itself is the subject.
- A quoted date, year or timestamp is visible text only; never use it to infer when the work was made or which era its style belongs to.
- When the image has no text, leave text out of the caption entirely.

## TEXT_ZH
- 只引用简短、清楚可辨、显著且有助于理解画面的文字，保持原字形与原标点。
- 不要翻译，也不要把非拉丁文字转写成拼音或罗马字。
- 引文保留原标点与阅读顺序。看不清的文字直接省略，不猜缺字，不添加 [无法辨认] 占位符。
- 密集对白、多处对话气泡、重复标签或背景小字，只有影响画面构成时才用一个短句概括其存在；不抄写或复述内容，不逐个数气泡，也不逐块罗列。文字本身就是主体时，最多引用一处简短、显著且清楚可辨的文字。
- 除非文字本身就是画面主体，否则不描述字体与排版。
- 引用的日期、年份或时间戳只作为可见文字，不用于推断作品创作年代或画风年代。
- 整张图没有任何文字时，整段不要提文字。
